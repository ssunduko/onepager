Salesmessage Style Guide
Source: Figma design system (One-Pagers file, SMS Credit Calculation Guide, 2026-10-13)

Typography
Page hero headlines: Product Sans Bold, letter-spacing -1% (Inter Bold when Product Sans is not installed)
Section headings: Inter Bold
Labels, table headers, questions: Inter Semi Bold
Body Text: Inter Regular

Colors
Salesmsg Blue (accent, headline highlight, values, table headers, page numbers): #0180f4
Topic pill text: #0072db (pill background is Salesmsg Blue at 10%)
Salesmsg Green CTA Button: #13cb6e
Headline and body text: #121517
Footer and meta text: #667085
Callout text: #023359
Cards, panels, callouts and striped rows: #f0f8ff
Hero band: radial gradient from white (left) to #c2e3ff (right)
Background: #ffffff

Layout
Hero band at the top of every page: logo, date, pill topic label, two-tone headline, subtitle.
Rounded panels (10pt cards, 7pt rows), no divider rules.
Footer: logo, website / phone, social icons, page number in blue.
